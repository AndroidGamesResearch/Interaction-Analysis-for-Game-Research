# Analysis Results

This folder contains the principal outputs generated by the complete empirical analysis script.

## Dataset Overview

### `dataset_overview.png`

Displays the structure of the dataset used in the analysis:

- 1,778,700 observations
- 11 variables
- 3,025 unique games
- 10 genres
- 42 national markets
- 14 monthly periods

It also reports the variables used in the empirical analysis.

---

## Stage 1 — Genre-Level Interaction Analysis

### `stage_1_genre_level_pooled_output.png`

Reports the pooled Ads/IAP × genre interaction results for both:

- install success
- rating success

The output includes DDP and RoR estimates, 95% confidence intervals, direction on each interaction scale, and the resulting joint interaction classification.

---

## Stage 2 — Cross-Market Analysis

### `stage_2_cross_market_output.png`

Reports the cross-market interaction results across the 42 national markets for both install success and rating success.

The output includes mean country-level DDP and RoR estimates and the number of countries exhibiting each joint interaction pattern.

---

## Stage 3 — Temporal Analysis

### `stage_3_temporal_output.png`

Reports the temporal interaction results across country–month contexts for both install success and rating success.

For each monetization × genre relationship, the output summarizes mean country–month DDP and RoR estimates and the distribution of joint interaction patterns across the 588 possible country–month contexts (42 markets × 14 months).

---

## Stage 4 — Gameplay Characterization

Stage 4 consists of game selection and manual gameplay characterization rather than a single statistical output table.

The corresponding methodology, selected-game lists, game-selection outputs, characterization materials, and supporting documentation are provided in the gameplay-characterization folder under `supplementary/`.

---

## Stage 5 — National-Context Analysis

### `stage_5_national_context_output.png`

Reports the national-context analysis for the five retained positive interaction profiles.

The output contains the 25 Spearman correlations between country-specific DDP and the five national-context indicators, together with raw p-values, Benjamini–Hochberg FDR-adjusted p-values, and the associations remaining significant after FDR correction.

---

## Consolidated Summary

### `consolidated_summary_output.png`

Provides a compact summary of the interaction results from Stages 1–3.

For each Ads/IAP × genre relationship, the table combines:

- pooled DDP and RoR estimates;
- pooled interaction direction;
- country-level interaction classifications; and
- country–month interaction classifications.

Results are reported separately for install success and rating success.
